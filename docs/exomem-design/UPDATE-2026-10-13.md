# Exomem design update, rounds 41–45: implementation guide

**For:** the agent building Exomem's web experience (Codex, Claude Code or another), and the operator.
**Base:** the design handoff already in the substrate repo, `docs/exomem-design/` (commit `d15b099`, 11 Oct 2026). Everything there still holds unless this brief changes it.
**Status:** every decision below was made with the operator in review rounds 41–43 (13 Oct 2026). Where something is still open, it says so.

The order of authority is the same as before: **boards win for visuals and copy, the READMEs win for rules and behaviour, the real code wins for capability**. If a design needs something the backend can't do, stop and tell the operator. Don't fake it.

---

## 1. Brand assets are first-class

`brand/exomem-brand-kit/` is the full kit: the mark (Dot E), the wordmark (the logo), the lockups, favicons and app icons, the OG image, the six email bands, and the living mark as a React component. Rules are in its README.

- **Never redraw** the mark or the wordmark. Use these SVGs. PNGs are renders of them.
- The `-on-light` files were recoloured on 13 Oct to the locked light palette (ink `#1A1814`, amber `#A86E00`). The first kit is in `brand/_archive/` and must be kept.
- **Store it in three places:** Google Drive (`Exomem / Brand / exomem-brand-kit`), the substrate repo (`docs/exomem-design/brand/` for the source, `public/exomem/` for the files the site serves), and keep the archive zip with it. Ask the operator before pushing to the repo.
- Head tags and file names are in the kit's README.
- Still to make, after launch: the inbox logo (BIMI). It needs DMARC at `p=quarantine` or `reject` and an SVG Tiny PS version of the mark.

## 2. Motion: the graphs are alive (round 41)

Reference: `engine/alive-engine.js` and `engine/ENGINE.md`. Boards: `R41-*`. Prototype: `prototypes/exomem-alive.html`.

- **Direction chosen:** "A + loop". At rest, ink pulses fill link by link, plus today's walking light as the idle state. Tide was liked but is too much; Grow was dropped.
- **Amber only for real events**, always with words: a recall, a save, a connection, a payment landing.
- **Landing:** hero, the three "how it works" diagrams, and the living map (it recalls a note and what it links to every few seconds, with the caption "Example: Claude recalls a note and what it links to").
- **Home:** the band's walker is ink. Real recalls and saves light it in amber, starting near the app tile that acted; that tile gets an amber line and an amber glyph, no glow. The live line says what happened ("Claude Code recalled 3 notes · 2 h ago"). Needs the activity feed (§9).
- **Start page:** the field walks; connecting lights it; paying makes amber pulses flow into the checkout panel; paid answers once, lighter than connecting.
- **Hover and taps:** hover follows the mouse at any width (a narrow desktop window too). On touch, a tap lights the closest note and its neighbours for about 1.8 s; ignore the browser's follow-up mouse events for 2 s after a tap. Pointer position includes the page's scroll.
- **Graph drawing:** SVG, at today's line weight. Links stop at each note's edge.
- **Light mode is on par with dark:** moving ink matches dark mode's contrast, not its opacity. Fix the lit-note bug (system/CHANGES §1).
- **Reduced motion:** nothing travels; events light in place.

## 4. The subscribe step: Paddle inline (round 42)

Boards: `R42-Checkout-States` (every state × desktop/phone × dark/light × price), `R42-Checkout-Live`, `R42-Summary-Layouts` (B, "price first", is the one), the component `Checkout-Inline`. Prototype: `prototypes/exomem-checkout.html` (its buttons play the rare cases).

- **One surface:** our summary and Paddle's frame in one panel with a hairline between. Desktop: two columns (summary, then Paddle's frame at 476 px). Phone: summary first, then the frame. **This page may scroll**, and the field scrolls with it (it stays at the top with the header).
- **Paddle.js:** `displayMode: inline`, `variant: one-page`, `theme` = the site theme, `frameInitialHeight: 450`, transparent `frameStyle`, email prefilled from the account, `showAddDiscounts: false` (there are no discount codes; the friend price comes from the invitation), `showAddTaxId: true`.
- **States and the events that drive them:**

| State | From | What the page does |
|---|---|---|
| Loading | frame opened | Summary labels with blanks held at their final width; the frame's 450 px held |
| Ready | `checkout.loaded` | Every number from the event; `checkout.updated` redraws in place |
| Paying | `checkout.payment.initiated` | "Paying now" in amber; amber pulses flow into the panel; on a phone the status line also sits under Paddle's button. **The E is a progress meter tied to real events:** each row is a step (sent, bank, paid, switching on, ready); done rows are amber, the current row beats, later rows are ink |
| Slow bank | 8 s after Paying with no answer | "Still waiting for your bank. This can take a minute. Keep this tab open." |
| Payment failed | `checkout.payment.failed` | "Nothing was charged." Paddle explains inside its frame. No red on our side |
| Paid, almost ready | `checkout.completed`, our server not yet active | The left says "Subscribed · next payment {date}". The right shows three steps: ✓ Payment received by Paddle · ● Switching on your Exomem · ○ Then you add it to your assistant. The big E scans; the button's place is kept |
| Still setting up | 30 s in Almost ready | "We'll email {email} when it's ready", so they can close the tab |
| Paid | our server says active | The E sweeps to full amber and stays; the field answers once in amber; "Add it to your assistant →" |
| Paddle didn't load | `checkout.error`, or no `checkout.loaded` in 15 s | "Paddle's form didn't load. A content blocker or a weak connection can stop it. Nothing was charged." and Try again. The summary keeps its blanks (no numbers without Paddle) |

- **Coming back** after closing the tab shows whatever is true then: setting up, or ready.
- **Local prices:** charge in the buyer's currency. EUR base with Paddle price overrides for USD and GBP at round amounts; automatic conversion for the rest. The boards use €15 / $15 / £13 (friend €10 / $10 / £9) as **samples; the operator confirms the real amounts**. Format with `Intl.NumberFormat` and the currency's own decimals.
- **Money format:** prices people read (the big price, sentences) drop `.00` when whole ("€15 a month", "Paying €15 with Paddle"); the ledger rows (VAT, Due today) keep two decimals so the column lines up.
- **Tax:** Europe shows "VAT included"; the US adds sales tax as its own line ("Plus sales tax, shown below"); an EU business with a VAT number gets "VAT 0%, reverse charge" and pays the price without VAT.
- **Paddle's own fields** are styled in Paddle's dashboard to match ours: `paddle/exomem-paddle-styling-brief.md` has the values for both modes and the ground rules.
- **Every number comes from Paddle at run time.** The board's numbers are samples.

## 6. Every remaining case (round 43)

Boards on the canvas page "Round 43 · Every case". All are real component states (props listed in each board's `data-props`).

- **Start page** (`Journey-Page`): `linkexpired` ("Sign-in links work once, for 15 minutes.") and `linkused`: no form, one button, "Email me a new link" (the link tells us the address), `ratelimited` (the button shows the wait, dimmed; same words for every address), `waitlist` (with a quiet "Wrong address? Change it" for typos), `unreachable`.
- **Out of reach, the graphs say so too:** the field and Home's band hold still, take no hover, and their links fall to a faint trace (connections cut).
- **When Exomem is off or out of reach, the logo's lit row goes ink** (header wordmark and Home's mark): unreachable, ended, paused, suspended, expired.
- **Can't reach Exomem, one pattern:** with nothing to show, the page says it (Start). With something to show, keep what was there, held still and greyed where it was live, with the time it is from, a banner and Try now (Home `reach: lost`, Viewer `reach: lost`). Retry every 8 s at first, then slower, up to a minute. Never "error", never a code.
- **First day:** Home `fresh` (no notes yet) and `fresh` + `firstNote`.
- **Viewer search uses the same retrieval as the assistants** (titles and text, by words and by meaning). `start: noresults` says what was searched and what to try; `vault: empty` (no relation chips, counts or review count: there are no relations yet), `vault: large` (draws the most-linked notes, the rest as dust, "400 of 4,213", zoom or search for the rest; the side panel's numbers in that board are still the sample vault's).

## 6b. Decided in round 44

- **Checkout density: A**, as built. the operator sees no scroll on his screens either way; `density` is removed from `Checkout-Inline` and the click-through.
- **The writing E (E2) is the progress mark everywhere.** Boards `R44-Writing-E` (dark) and `R44-Writing-E-Light`. The E's 18 dots (10 in the small cut) are ordered the way a pen draws it; a process with N steps splits that stroke (up to five steps end on the letter's corners: 2 → 11/18, 3 → 7/11/18, 4 → 5/9/13/18, 5 → 5/7/11/13/18; more split it evenly). The running step has a wave along it; no count = a comet runs the whole stroke; a percentage fills the stroke; done = all lit plus one seal ripple (halo in light); failure un-writes, then rests; off = no amber. The same states work in the wordmark's E. Implementation: three CSS numbers registered with `@property` (`--w` written, `--b` the pen's end, `--rest`), so the browser animates every change; exact CSS in `boards/Checkout-Inline.dc.html` and the component library (`Mark`, `Wordmark`, `Progress`). It replaces CHANGES §7 (the row-scan E) and the separate keyframes of the brand kit's `ExomemMark.tsx`.
- **No state flashes.** Checkout: Paddle's frame stays in place and the loading, unavailable and paid views cross-fade over it (the panel keeps its height); the declined message opens to its height; titles, the status line, "Due today / Paying now / Paid today" and the button label cross-fade; the line's E slides in. Use the library's `Swap`, `Crossfade`, `Collapse`, `FadeIn` for every state change in the product.
- **Status page:** Upptime on GitHub Actions and Pages, free and off our servers. Plan, config and theme in `status/`.
- **Component library:** the Exomem Design System artifact (link in `design-system/README.md`), with the same files in `design-system/`.

## 6c. Round 45: from the operator's walkthrough

Board `R45-Review` lists each point with before and after.

- **Text selection is ours:** `::selection` uses `selection-bg` (amber at 30%) under `selection-fg` (ink) in both themes, on every surface. Never the browser's blue.
- **Notes are solid; lines stop at a note's edge, everywhere.** The engine trims every line that ends on a note (`trimSvg` in `engine/alive-engine.js`), the start-page field trims its moving links too, and the viewer mixes a note's strength into its colour (`color-mix`) instead of using element opacity. Build rule: never draw a note with `opacity` over links; mix the colour or stop the link.
- **Phone hero:** the question gets its own line under "Claude Code · 120 ms" and is the full question, as on desktop.
- **Phone checkout:** after paying, the form's space closes up to the paid view (animated) and the paid view scrolls into sight. Desktop keeps the panel height.
- **Connect:** choosing Claude or ChatGPT opens it in a new tab (Claude: Settings → Connectors; ChatGPT: chatgpt.com/plugins) and our page waits; "Open … again ↗" reopens it. Replace these with the exact deep links to Exomem's listing once Exomem is in each directory.
- **The writing E** is the `Writing-E` component; both Round 44 boards import it (top-level boards don't play timers on the canvas). Its "Try any transition" panel, and the library's Mark card, show every state change.
- **Round 46 (board `R46-Review`):** the writing E is driven by a small per-frame script (`weDrive` in `Writing-E` and `Checkout-Inline`) rather than CSS transitions on custom properties, so it animates in any viewer; the product can use either. The hero question comes from Claude. Phone hover labels never cover another label or leave the card. The living map has no caption. The phone paid view closes up to its content. A failure un-writes the E and the logo rests; grey stays reserved for "can't reach Exomem".
- **Round 47 (board `R47-Review`):** the desktop supersedes arrow ends just outside its note. The landing names four apps plus a dashed "+ any MCP app" pill. Signed in, the wordmark goes Home on every flow page, and the choose page has "Go to Home". Phone flow pages sit higher. After connecting: "Add another app". "Keep it as it is" goes back to the list (no second celebration). Sign-in copy says the email is what the Exomem is tied to.
- **Add-ons (decision for the build):** no checkout for an add-on to a running subscription. Preview the change with Paddle (prorated), show one confirm ("Add 10 GB · €X now"), update the subscription; on a declined card open Paddle's inline form in the same panel. Invoices, card changes and cancelling go to Paddle's customer portal. Check the exact Paddle Billing calls first.
- Open: the viewer reader on phones replaces the map, as on desktop. A strip of the note's neighbourhood above the reader is offered, not drawn.

## 7. Accessibility (round 43)

Board: `R43-Accessibility`.

- **Forced colours:** buttons and fields keep a system border; focus rings have a transparent outline the system paints (CHANGES §4).
- **Zoom 200% and a phone held sideways:** the layout follows the width, so a 640 px or 844 × 390 window gets the phone layout, centred. Phone flow pages **may scroll when the window is shorter than the layout**. This replaces "phone flow pages never scroll" for short windows only; normal phones (640–932 px tall) still fit without scrolling, except the checkout.
- **Screen readers:** one polite live region per page for status lines; a new step moves focus to its heading; paid moves focus to "Add it to your assistant"; Paddle didn't load moves focus to Try again; motion is never announced. The full table is on the board.

## 7b. One place for the logo

The wordmark is 17 px tall with its top edge 29 px from the top of the page, on every page, desktop and phone (landing, start, checkout, Home). Horizontally it follows each page's content edge (40 px on product pages, the landing's own column on the landing, 16 px on phones).

## 9. What the backend must provide (new in 41–43)

Check each against the code before building on it. If it doesn't exist, stop and propose the smallest change.

| Need | For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Activity feed: recent recalls and saves with app, time, count | Home's amber events and live line | Polling is fine; a server-sent stream is better. Without it, Home shows the ink walker only |
| "Subscription active" for the signed-in account, after `checkout.completed` | Paid, almost ready → Paid | Paddle webhook updates our record; the page polls a small endpoint |
| Sign-in link outcome: expired or already used | Link expired / used | Must not reveal whether an account exists |
| Health endpoints: control `/healthz`, `/healthz/email`; cluster `/healthz` (canary read and write) | The status page | Under 1 s, no auth, `{"ok":true}` or 503; say nothing about accounts. See `status/STATUS-PAGE.md` |
| Resend limit with the wait in minutes | Too many links | Same answer for every address |
| Invitation-only gate and a waitlist record | Waitlist page and email W1 | |
| A status page, hosted away from our own servers | "Our status page says why" (connect error, can't reach) | Doesn't exist yet. A hosted status service is quickest; it must stay up when we're down |
| A cheap reachability check | Can't reach Exomem | Distinguish "our server" from "your connection" only if it's reliable; otherwise say both |
| Paddle prices with USD and GBP overrides; `showAddTaxId` | Local prices, reverse charge | the operator sets amounts |
