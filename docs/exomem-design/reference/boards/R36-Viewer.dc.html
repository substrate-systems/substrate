The graph viewer
Built on Shneiderman’s mantra: overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand. Plus the two Exomem moves: ask why a note is true, and review where notes disagree. The live board above (The graph viewer) is clickable.
Desktop · overview first, then zoom and filter, then detailsScroll to zoom (from far out to close in), drag to move; the square button brings everything back. Closer in, more notes name themselves. Lines carry their type: amber supports, dotted refines, dashed contradicts. Every relation type can be shown or hidden. Hover a note and each of its links names its relation; click it for the details, then read the whole note. Live activity can be paused, and the timeline replays at half, normal or double speed.

why is this true? The trail lights up: sources and the notes that back it

Desktop · reviewing disagreementsExomem notices two notes that may disagree and never picks a side. You choose: one supersedes the other, both hold, not a disagreement, or later. Every choice can be undone.
review The pair, side by side, with dates and sources
after a decision The next pair comes up; Undo stays in reach
