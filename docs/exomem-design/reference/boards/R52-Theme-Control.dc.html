Round 52 · The theme control, decided
The button shows your theme; a tap opens Auto, Light, Dark
Our own three glyphs from round 50: the half circle for Auto, the sun, the moon. Not Lucide: the sun and moon are drawn to sit with the Dot E and the mono type. The button shows the theme in force. A tap opens a small menu, and picking a row switches the page and closes it.
StatesAuto (follows your device, the default), Light, Dark. The label in the menu is “Auto”.
The buttonThe glyph of the theme in force: half circle, sun or moon. 15 px glyph, fg-secondary, in a round 36 px target (40 px on phones) that fills with bg-hover on hover or press.
The menubg-raised, 1 px border-card, r-pop, shadow-pop, 4 px padding. Rows 36 px (44 px on phones): glyph, word, ✓ on the current one, which also sits on bg-hover. It opens under the button, aligned to its left edge, and stays on screen.
BehaviourPicking a row applies at once and closes. Escape or a tap outside closes. No tooltip, no toast.
First paintUnchanged: data-theme-choice on before the page paints; the three glyphs are in the HTML and CSS shows the one in force.

How it behaves
Keyboard: Enter or Space opens the menu with the current row focused; Up and Down move; Enter picks; Escape closes and returns focus to the button. Tab closes it too.
Screen readers: the button reads “Theme: Auto” (or Light, or Dark), with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded. Rows are menuitemradio with aria-checked.
Motion: the menu fades in over dur-hover (150 ms), 4 px up; reduced motion: it simply appears. The page’s theme change itself is instant.
The glyphs, as code
currentColor, so they take the button’s colour in both themes.
