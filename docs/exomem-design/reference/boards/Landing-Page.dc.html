01 · How it stays true

Built to change without forgetting.

01Remember

Every assistant you connect reads from it and adds to it. Ask in your own words; decisions, sources and what you learned are kept, not lost when the chat ends.

02Connect

Notes link by what they mean: this supports that, this contradicts that. Contradictions come up for review instead of one answer quietly winning.

03Evolve

Nothing is thrown away. When you change your mind, the old note is superseded and kept, so you can still ask what you knew in March, and why.

02 · See it

Your knowledge, as a living map.

In your Exomem account: click any note to see what supports it, what it replaced and where it came from. Scrub back in time. Watch your assistants recall, live.
