LoadingBefore Paddle’s frame is ready. Our summary shows its labels with the numbers held as blanks of the same width; the frame’s space is held at 450 px. Nothing moves when it arrives.
ReadyNumbers from Paddle’s checkout.loaded. Changing the country fires checkout.updated: VAT and totals redraw in place, in fixed-width slots.
PayingFrom checkout.payment.initiated. Paddle shows its own progress in its button. We say “Keep this tab open” (on a phone, right under the frame). The E beside the price writes itself: the top bar (sent to Paddle) is written, the pen runs the next stroke (your bank approves), and the field works: ink pulses flow into the panel from the notes around it, until Paddle answers.
Slow bankAfter 8 seconds with no answer, the line changes to “Still waiting for your bank. This can take a minute. Keep this tab open.” The E keeps working; nothing else moves.
Payment failedFrom checkout.payment.failed. Paddle explains inside its frame; our line stays calm and short: “Nothing was charged.” No red on our side.
Paid, setting upcheckout.completed has arrived, our server hasn’t switched the account on yet (usually a second or two). The left says Subscribed; the right holds the frame’s space, the big E writes the switching-on stroke and the button’s place is kept. After 30 seconds: “Paid. Still setting up. We’ll email you when it’s ready, so you can close this tab.” Coming back to the page later shows whichever is true then: setting up, or ready.
PaidFrom checkout.completed. We take over the frame’s space, keep the receipt numbers on the left, and hand over to “Add Exomem to your assistant”. The E writes its last stroke, lights whole and seals once; the field answers once, in amber, lighter than connecting.
Paddle didn’t loadcheckout.error, or no checkout.loaded after 15 seconds (a content blocker, a weak connection). There are no numbers without Paddle, so the summary keeps its blanks, held still. “Try again” loads the frame again. Nothing was charged.
Tax and buyersPaddle decides per buyer (tax_mode: location) and shows local prices where we set them (USD, GBP; samples). Europe: the price includes VAT. The US: the price is before tax, sales tax is added and shown as its own line, and Due today is the total. A business with an EU VAT number: no VAT (reverse charge), so the monthly charge is the price without VAT. The rows keep their slots in every case.

How the two read as one page
One surfaceSummary and frame sit in one panel with one border, divided by a hairline. Paddle’s frame has a transparent background, so its fields stand on our panel colour. Paddle’s theme follows ours (dark or light). Below the panel, one line: Paddle takes the payment and sends the receipts.
Desktop, 960 px and upTwo columns in a 940 px panel: the summary takes the rest, Paddle’s frame gets 476 px (its minimum is 312). Summary on the left because people read what they buy before they pay. Heading and line above, centred, as on every other step.
768 to 959 pxSame two columns; the frame narrows to 380 px, the summary to what’s left. The panel keeps 24 px from each edge.
Phone, 360 to 430 pxOne column in one panel: the summary first (the price must be seen before paying), a hairline, then Paddle’s frame. The “what happens next” list is left out; the line under the heading says it. The whole page scrolls, field included: the graph stays up top with the header and leaves with it.
Reserved spaceEvery number sits in a fixed-width, tabular slot, so a new VAT rate redraws in place. The status line keeps its height when empty. The frame’s space is held at 450 px before Paddle is ready, and the paid message fills the same space.
AmberOnly for what is live or current: the friend-price label (the invitation applies now), the current step in the list, the paid dot and the paid E (the account is live), and while paying the writing E beside the price (the payment is what is happening now). Not on Paddle’s button, not on errors. The walking light rests in ink during checkout; the one amber event is the field answering “paid”.
Paddle’s requirementsOn the page: what is bought (Exomem, monthly), the recurring price and when it renews, subtotal, tax and total with currency, and Paddle’s whole frame including its footer with terms and privacy. Our refund policy link only if it differs from Paddle’s.
Every numberFrom Paddle at run time: checkout.loaded and checkout.updated carry the items, totals, tax and recurring totals, in the buyer’s currency. Format with the currency’s own decimals, not toFixed(2). The next payment date is computed from today. The board’s numbers are samples and say so.
EmailPrefilled from the Exomem account. Paddle shows it in its frame; we don’t repeat it on our side.
After paidcheckout.completed shows our paid state at once. If the server’s webhook hasn’t confirmed the subscription yet, the button waits with “Setting up your Exomem” (the old Confirming step, now in place); then it hands over to the choose step.
