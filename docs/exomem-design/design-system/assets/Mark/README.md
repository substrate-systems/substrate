# Mark

The Dot E alone is the icon: a 5 × 7 dot E with one lit middle row of four dots. In product UI, use the `Mark` component (it animates and draws the small cut by itself).

- `exomem-mark.svg` — on dark. Inks `#ECE9E2` and amber `#FFB000`.
- `exomem-mark-on-light.svg` — on light. Inks `#1A1814` and `#A86E00`.
- `exomem-mark-small.svg`, `-small-on-light.svg` — below 32 px: the small cut (10 dots, one even pitch).
- `exomem-mark-mono-white.svg` (single ink `#FFFFFF`), `exomem-mark-mono-black.svg` (single ink `#000000`) — single-ink print, embossing, watermarks only.

Amber marks the live part only: the middle row at rest, the whole E only when connected or done. The middle row has four dots, not five.
