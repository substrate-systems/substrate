# Change: Restyle Endstate commercial pages

## Why

The supporters and integration-sponsorship routes currently use a narrow reading
layout despite being decision and action surfaces. They should use the existing
Endstate landing-page grammar without changing their commercial terms or paths.

## What Changes

- Compose `/endstate/supporters` with a wide support hero, contained recognition
  roster, contribution surface, and opt-in closing note.
- Compose `/endstate/sponsor-an-integration` with a wide outcome hero,
  installation-versus-migration comparison, sponsorship-benefit cards, grouped
  public/private/maintenance boundaries, and a contained quote panel.
- Retain all existing checkout, mailto, server-rendering, supporter-fetch, and
  approved commercial-copy behaviour.

## Non-goals

- No changes to the Endstate landing page, articles, legal routes, payment
  identifiers, backend routes, data collection, prices, or commercial terms.
