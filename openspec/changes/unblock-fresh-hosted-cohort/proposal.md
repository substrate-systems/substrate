## Why

The final friends-only Hosted cohort cannot complete its paired Claude and OpenAI run: consuming the one-shot reviewer bootstrap seals its original client, while the credential gate rejects the fresh sibling clients that must take over. Promotion can also outlive its five-minute recorded route observation even while every routed cell is healthy.

## What Changes

- Allow a consumed reviewer bootstrap outcome to issue internal-canary credentials to fresh, attributed sibling staged clients for its exact tenant, candidate, assignment, and generation, without re-enabling or reusing the bootstrap client.
- Permit the exact paired Claude and OpenAI credentials for that assignment while retaining expiry, configured-client, assignment, candidate, and lineage fences.
- Make Hosted cohort promotion derive a fresh strict outer-v2 health observation for every currently routable cell, then atomically record the exact observations and refresh promotion authority before evaluating the existing cohort promotion transaction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `exomem-hosted-promotion-runtime`: Server-derived strict outer-v2 runtime health refresh for Hosted cohort promotion.

### Modified Capabilities

- `exomem-tenant-control-plane`: Cohort promotion must use freshly verified persisted cell runtime identity and route observations.
- `exomem-hosted-access`: Consumed reviewer bootstrap outcomes may authorize fresh sibling internal-canary clients with exact lineage.

## Impact

Changes reviewer credential issuance, Hosted cohort promotion authority, lifecycle/provisioner health reuse, focused PostgreSQL and paired acceptance coverage, and the Hosted alpha runbook.
